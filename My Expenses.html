Frequently Asked Questions

Does My Expenses require an internet connection?

No. My Expenses is completely offline-first. All transactions, categories, and settings are saved securely on your phone.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There are no logins, passwords, phone numbers, or email requirements to use the app.

How do I transfer my data to a new phone?

You own your data. Go to Settings > Backup & Restore > Export JSON Backup. Move the saved backup file to your new device and tap Import JSON Backup. You can also export to CSV for Google Sheets or Excel.

What happens when I delete an expense?

It is safely moved to the built-in Recycle Bin for 7 days. You can restore it with all details intact at any time during this period. After 7 days, it is permanently deleted.
